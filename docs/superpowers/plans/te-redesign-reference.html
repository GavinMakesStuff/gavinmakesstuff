01 / terralens

Filling in ESG Gaps in Mining

Product designData platformB2B SaaS

TerraLens is an ESG data platform designed for resource-constrained junior mining companies to organize disclosures and close reporting gaps that larger operators solve with dedicated compliance teams.

I led the product design end to end — mapping the reporting workflow with two mining operators, structuring the data model behind their disclosures, and designing the dashboard that turns scattered spreadsheets into an auditable ESG record.

08.04

How I Built a Personal Job Search Tool to Get Through a Career Change

When I started actively job searching after a career change, I was drowning in tabs — thirty postings open at once, half of them re-reads, no way to tell which ones actually fit until I'd already written a cover letter.

So I built Scout: a locally hosted dashboard that pulls in postings and scores them against my own resume and history, so the tabs I keep open are the ones actually worth writing for.
