2. How We Use It

  • To provide the core Service: running your analyses and returning results.
  • To operate your account, token balance, and subscription.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.
  • To detect and prevent abuse (e.g., free-tier limit circumvention).
  • To produce combined, aggregate statistics about how Scout is used (for example, how many people reach the free limit and later buy tokens). These never identify you.
  • If you choose to join the optional Insights program, to produce aggregate statistics as described in section 11.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your resume or job-posting content to train any AI model.

Aggregate statistics. We may share combined, de-identified statistics about how Scout is used (for example, how many people use the free tier or which kinds of jobs are analyzed most), based on groups of 10 or more users, with investors and business partners. We may also provide employers, in exchange for a fee, combined statistics that are based only on information from people who joined the Insights program (section 11), again for groups of 10 or more. These statistics never include names, email addresses, notes, contact details, resume or cover-letter text, job-posting text or links, or any single person's information, and we do not give any employer information about individual applicants or users.

4. Data Retention

We keep your account and profile data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email gavinmakesstuff@gmail.com and we'll process the request manually. A self-serve delete option may be added in the future. Some records (e.g., transaction history) may be retained longer where needed for accounting, tax, or legal purposes. Data from the optional Insights program is handled as described in section 11.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Scout uses your browser's local storage to keep track of things like saved/applied jobs and preferences on your device. We don't currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information (e.g., encrypted connections, access-controlled database, no direct browser access to our AI or payment provider keys). No system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email gavinmakesstuff@gmail.com. If you joined the Insights program, you can also turn it off at any time under "Help improve Scout" on your profile page.

8. International Users

Scout is intended for users in North America. Your information is processed in Canada (Supabase) and may be processed by our other providers (Stripe, Anthropic, Vercel) in the United States or elsewhere they operate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll make reasonable efforts to let existing users know.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email gavinmakesstuff@gmail.com.

11. Optional Insights Program

Insights program version 2026-10-22

The Insights program is optional and off by default. The program information described in this section is not collected unless you turn it on under "Help improve Scout" on your profile page, and you can turn it off at any time. (Separately, section 1 describes records Scout keeps for everyone in order to run the service.)

What is collected in this version. At the moment the program records only that you opened Scout. Over time we may add the kinds of information listed below. We will update this section and ask you again before we collect anything new.

What we may collect over time if you join. Grouped, standardized details, not the text you typed:

  • About you: grouped details such as your experience range, the category of role and industry you are looking for, work-mode preference, province/state or country, pay range, categories of credentials and skills, and employment status and education level if you choose to answer.
  • About the jobs you analyze: the role category, industry, level, pay range, work mode, where the posting was found, the match score, and which skills were missing.
  • What you do in Scout and when, rounded to the day or week: for example analyzing, saving or applying to a job, and updating an application's status, interviews and final decision.
  • Optional answers you choose to give, such as why you declined an offer, an offered pay range, or why you cancelled a subscription.

What this program never collects. Your name, email address, notes, contact details, resume or cover-letter text, job-posting text or links, exact location or IP address, or payment details.

How it is stored. Program data is tied to a random code and not to your account. The link between the two is kept in a restricted, access-controlled part of our database. Access is limited to the operator of Scout and to our database provider (Supabase), which acts as our service provider. No other user, and no employer, can see it. Like every request to Scout, the request that delivers this information is also logged briefly against your account for abuse protection. That log is not part of the program data, and the part of it that relates to the program is deleted when you turn the program off or after one day.

Turning it off or deleting your account. Turning the program off stops collection and deletes the link between your account and your program information. After that, nothing in the program connects the earlier information to you, and we do not try to re-link it. Deleting your account does the same. Because other records, such as purchase dates, also exist, we cannot promise that information could never be matched to a person, which is one reason we only report combined statistics for groups of 10 or more. Combined statistics already produced do not identify anyone and may remain. If you turn the program off, you can turn it back on after 30 days.

How it is used. To produce combined statistics for groups of 10 or more people, which may be shared as described in section 3. We never share information about an individual.

Thank-you tokens. The first time you turn the program on, we add 3 tokens to your account. This happens once per account; turning it off and on again does not earn more. Accounts that the operator designates as internal, such as VIP or administrator accounts, are not eligible.

Each time you change your choice we record the date and the version of this section that applied.
