Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Scout collects, why, and how it's handled. Scout is operated by Gavin Krohman, an individual sole proprietor based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada ("we," "us," "our").

1. What We Collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account infoEmail address, authentication dataTo create and secure your account (via Supabase)
Profile infoRole, target industry, salary expectations, experience, certifications, skills, career notes you enterUsed to personalize job-posting analysis against your profile
Resume contentText extracted from a resume you uploadTo build/update your profile, at your request
Job posting textText you paste in for analysisSent for AI analysis to generate your results
Usage & billing recordsToken balance, analyses run, subscription status, transaction historyTo operate the token/subscription system and provide support
Payment infoHandled entirely by StripeWe never receive or store your full card number

2. How We Use It

  • To provide the core Service: running your analyses and returning results.
  • To operate your account, token balance, and subscription.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.
  • To detect and prevent abuse (e.g., free-tier limit circumvention).

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your resume or job-posting content to train any AI model.

3. Who We Share It With

We use a small number of service providers ("subprocessors") to run Scout. We don't sell your data to anyone else.

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseAuthentication and database hosting (project region: Canada Central)
AnthropicProcesses your job-posting/profile/resume text via the Claude API to generate your analysis, under Anthropic's standard API terms
StripePayment processing for subscriptions and token purchases
VercelApplication hosting

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Scout or our users.

4. Data Retention

We keep your account and profile data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email gavinmakesstuff@gmail.com and we'll process the request manually. A self-serve delete option may be added in the future. Some records (e.g., transaction history) may be retained longer where needed for accounting, tax, or legal purposes.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Scout uses your browser's local storage to keep track of things like saved/applied jobs and preferences on your device. We don't currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information (e.g., encrypted connections, access-controlled database, no direct browser access to our AI or payment provider keys). No system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email gavinmakesstuff@gmail.com.

8. International Users

Scout is intended for users in North America. Your information is processed in Canada (Supabase) and may be processed by our other providers (Stripe, Anthropic, Vercel) in the United States or elsewhere they operate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll make reasonable efforts to let existing users know.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email gavinmakesstuff@gmail.com.

This document is a plain-language starting point and hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer.
