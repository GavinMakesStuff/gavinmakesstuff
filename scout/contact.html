Billing and tokens

What happens when I run out of tokens?

Free accounts get 3 analyses a week, resetting every Monday. Plus, Pro, and pay-as-you-go accounts draw from a token balance instead: if you use it all before your next billing date, you can top up with a one-time token pack anytime, or just wait for your next month's renewal to add more. Nothing is auto-charged beyond your regular subscription price.

Do unused tokens expire?

No. Tokens never expire, whether they came from a subscription or a one-time top-up. Switching plans, cancelling, or letting a month pass doesn't wipe your balance.

What happens if I switch between Plus and Pro?

Your plan updates immediately with no extra charge and no double billing. Your existing token balance carries over untouched, and the new price plus the new plan's monthly token amount take effect starting your next regular billing date. For example: you're on Plus (40 tokens/mo) with 15 left, and you switch to Pro on day 10 of your cycle. You keep using those 15 tokens under Pro for free until your next billing date, when you're charged the Pro price and get 100 fresh tokens added to whatever's left.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time from the Buy Tokens panel inside the app. Your plan stays active through the rest of your current billing period, then moves you to pay-as-you-go: no more monthly refill, but any tokens already in your balance stay usable for as long as you want, with no expiry.

When does my free weekly limit reset?

Every Monday, on a fixed weekly schedule. It's not a rolling 7 days from when you signed up.

Common questions

I want a refund on a subscription or token purchase.

Email me with your account email and what happened. Refunds are handled case-by-case, but I'll do my best to make it right.

I want my account and data deleted.

Email me a deletion request from your account's email address and I'll process it manually. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Something's broken.

Let me know what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.

I have a feature idea.

Send it over. Scout is actively developed and a lot of what exists today started as a user suggestion.
